# Phase 2 User Guide: AI-Powered Microsite Factory

## Welcome to MicroSite Forge!

Phase 2 brings you a complete AI-powered microsite generation platform. This guide will walk you through creating professional lead-generating microsites from start to finish.

## 🚀 Quick Start Overview

In just 4 steps, you can have a live microsite generating leads:

1. **Research** a profitable niche using AI
2. **Generate** SEO-optimized content
3. **Edit & Approve** content for quality
4. **Deploy** your live microsite

---

## 📊 Step 1: Niche Research

### Access the Research Dashboard

1. Log in to MicroSite Forge
2. Click **"Niche Research"** tab
3. Fill out the research form

### Research Parameters

| Field | Description | Example |
|-------|-------------|---------|
| **Niche** | Main business/service | plumbers, electricians, dentists |
| **Target Audience** | Who you're serving | homeowners, businesses, students |
| **Geography** | Location focus | local, New York, California |
| **Competitor Analysis** | Include competitor insights | ✅ Enabled |

### What You'll Get

After research completes:

- **📈 Keyword Intelligence**: Search volumes, competition levels, CPC data
- **🔍 Trending Topics**: What's hot in your niche right now
- **💡 Content Ideas**: 10+ proven content opportunities
- **🏢 Domain Suggestions**: Available domains with pricing
- **📊 Competitor Insights**: What others are doing right/wrong

### Pro Tips

- Start broad, then narrow down: "plumbers" → "emergency plumbers NYC"
- Check multiple geographies for local opportunities
- Look for "low-medium" competition keywords under 500K searches
- Use trending topics for timely content ideas

## ✍️ Step 2: Content Generation

### From Research Results

1. Review research findings
2. Click **"Content Gen"** tab
3. System auto-generates 5-page microsites:

### Your Generated Site Structure

```
/ (Homepage) - Service overview & value proposition
/services/ - Detailed service descriptions
/about/ - Trust-building company story
/contact/ - Lead capture forms
/blog/ - SEO content for rankings
```

### Content Features

- **SEO-Optimized**: Keywords naturally incorporated
- **Mobile-Friendly**: Responsive design
- **Lead Capture**: Contact forms built-in
- **Social Proof**: Testimonials, certifications
- **Clear CTAs**: Book now, call today buttons

---

## 🔧 Step 3: Content Editor

### Quality Assurance Workflow

1. Click **"Preview"** to see live rendering
2. Click **"Edit"** to modify individual pages
3. Review **SEO Scores** (aim for 70+)

### Editing Features

#### Editor Mode
- **Title Editing**: Optimize for click-through rates
- **Content Refinement**: Add, remove, restructure sections
- **Keyword Adjustment**: Fine-tune SEO terms
- **Meta Description**: Craft compelling previews

#### Quality Validation
- ✅ Keyword in title/first paragraph
- ✅ Minimum 500 words per page
- ✅ Proper heading hierarchy
- ✅ Meta descriptions under 160 chars

### Content Regeneration

- **Per Page**: Click "Regenerate" on any page
- **System Learning**: Improves based on your edits
- **Tone Adjustment**: Professional, casual, enthusiastic

---

## 🚀 Step 4: Site Deployment

### One-Click Deployment

1. Click **"Approve & Deploy"** (when score ≥70)
2. System creates:
   - **GitHub Repository**: Hugo static site
   - **Netlify Hosting**: Live website
   - **Custom Domain**: If specified
   - **SSL Certificate**: Automatic HTTPS

### Deployment Progress

- 🔄 **Generating**: AI content creation
- 🔄 **Building**: GitHub repository setup
- 🔄 **Deploying**: Netlify hosting
- ✅ **Live**: Your site is ready!

### Post-Deployment

- **Live URL**: `your-site-name.netlify.app`
- **Admin Panel**: Manage settings
- **Analytics**: Track traffic & leads
- **Custom Domain**: Point your domain here

## 🆘 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Research Not Working**
- Check internet connection
- Verify API keys are set
- Try simpler niche terms

**Content Quality Low**
- Add more specific details
- Use industry terminology
- Review competitor sites

**Deployment Failed**
- Check domain availability
- Ensure account has permissions
- Contact support with error messages

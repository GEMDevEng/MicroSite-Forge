## Key Features: End-to-End SOP Automation

MicroSite Forge maps directly to the Microsites Automation Operator SOP, automating every responsibility with AI-driven workflows. No more manual batches—it's all dashboard-triggered.

### 1. **AI-Powered Niche & Domain Research**
   - **Automated Discovery**: Enter a vertical (e.g., "epoxy flooring"), and our AI scans Google Autocomplete, Bing Suggestions, and SERPs for 1,000+ keyword clusters. Scores for volume, competition, and intent using integrated Keyword Surfer-like logic.
   - **Domain Sniper**: Real-time availability checks across 500+ TLDs (Cloudflare Registrar, Porkbun, Namecheap). Auto-purchases low-cost domains ($1–$2) or suggests premium EMDs. Avoids unreliable Freenom.
   - **Batch Scaling**: Generate 50–100 niche-location combos/week. Master dashboard logs everything: Service, Location, Domain, Projected ROI (based on AOV benchmarks like $2.5K–$6K/job for epoxy).
   - **Smart Filters**: Prioritize high-AOV verticals (e.g., garage epoxy, pool heaters) with low EMD competition. Includes 30-city launch lists for top metros (Phoenix, Miami, etc.).

### 2. **One-Click Website Setup & Deployment**
   - **Instant Site Builder**: Drag-and-drop Hugo-based templates generate static sites with 50+ hyper-targeted pages (neighborhood variants, FAQs, testimonials). Deploys to Netlify/Vercel free tiers in <30 seconds.
   - **Domain & DNS Magic**: Auto-connects domains via Cloudflare (SSL, CDN, performance tweaks for <2s loads). 99.9% uptime monitoring with instant alerts.
   - **GitHub Orchestration**: Backend pushes to your private repo via API; GitHub Actions trigger builds. Fork per client for white-labeling.
   - **SEO Booster**: Auto-generates sitemaps, submits to Google Search Console/Bing. Tracks indexing (target: 100% in 7–14 days) with velocity reports.

### 3. **Dynamic Content Generation & Optimization**
   - **AI Content Engine**: Powered by Grok 4 + OpenAI, generates 600–900 word Markdown pages per site. Prompts inject local relevance: "Trusted epoxy services in Scottsdale—same-day installs, UV-stable flakes."
   - **Page Structure Automation**: Builds SOP-compliant sections (Overview, Why Us, Testimonials, FAQs, CTA) with unique NAP footprints. Avoids thin content; adds visuals (stock before/afters via Unsplash API).
   - **CRO Iteration**: Monthly AI-driven tests (A/B headlines, CTAs) based on GSC impressions/CTR. Pro tier includes citations pack and NAP management across 50+ directories.

### 4. **Seamless Lead Capture & Intelligent Routing**
   - **Multi-Channel Capture**: Embeds customizable forms (Typeform/Tally integration) + unique Twilio numbers ($1/month/site). AI receptionist (Vapi/Retell) handles 24/7 calls: qualifies, transcribes, and routes.
   - **Smart Routing**: n8n workflows forward leads to client inboxes/SMS. Filters junk/dupes; scores quality (e.g., local homeowner intent).
   - **Tracking Hub**: Real-time dashboard shows call logs, recordings, and form submissions. Exclusivity enforcement: Blocks competitors in same ZIP/service.

### 5. **Automated Lead Delivery & Monetization**
   - **Branded Notifications**: Sends polished HTML emails on lead capture—logo, site link, details (Name, Phone, Query), and stats. Weekly summaries (Mondays) with totals and revenue projections.
   - **Invoicing Engine**: Auto-generates Google Docs/Stripe invoices from templates ($149 MRR + $75/lead). Handles flat-fee or performance pricing; annual prepay discounts.
   - **Payments & Analytics**: Stripe metered billing for add-ons. Dashboard KPIs: Leads/site, cost/lead, LTV:CAC (>5:1), churn alerts (<3.5%/month).

### 6. **Scaling Dashboard & Maintenance**
   - **Central Command Center**: Visualize 100+ sites/client with heatmaps (ranks, impressions, leads). "Run All" button scales batches: Add rows → Generate → Deploy → Notify.
   - **Proactive Maintenance**: AI monitors for downtime, rank drops, or low leads. Auto-QA: Replaces bad leads; runs CRO tweaks. Churn-proof ROI reports tie calls to jobs.
   - **Client Portal**: White-label access for end-users—view leads, recordings, invoices. Rate lead quality; request expansions (extra cities/services).

### 7. **Business Ops & Growth Tools**
   - **Outbound Accelerator**: Auto-generates personalized Looms/emails for 500 prospects (e.g., "Exclusive epoxy leads in Phoenix—first 3 free"). 90-day sequences with 7–10% reply rates.
   - **Proof Builder**: Spins up demo sites + call logs for sales. Tracks funnel: Demos booked, close rates (20–25%), CAC ($300–$600).
   - **Compliance Suite**: Enforces state call laws, NAP sync, no interlinking. Audit logs for Google compliance.
